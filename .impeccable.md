## Design Context

### Users
Dual audience: tech/ML recruiters who skim fast (need role, stack, and credibility signals at a glance) and ML/AI engineers who read deeply (care about project depth, architecture, and research quality). Both visit via share link or direct portfolio URL — no discovery flow.

### Brand Personality
Technically sharp, quietly ambitious, TRON-world aesthetic. Three words: **grid-precision, neon-depth, earned-credentials**. The TRON theme is intentional and core to identity — not a gimmick.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark mode. Deep navy/black backgrounds, neon cyan primary, neon pink accent, orange for timestamps/metadata. Orbitron for display headings, Share Tech Mono for body/code. Canvas-based animated TRON grid in the background. The visual tone is retro-futuristic: 80s sci-fi operating system.

Anti-references: generic "developer portfolio with particles", flat minimal light-mode portfolios, glassmorphism overload.

### Design Principles
1. **Scannability first** — recruiters need to identify EXPERIENCE, EDUCATION, SKILLS, PROJECTS immediately. TRON-speak subtitles (COMBAT HISTORY, etc.) are flavor, not labels.
2. **Hierarchy through contrast** — not everything is equally important. Featured info rows, asymmetric skill grids, and varied section weight signal what matters.
3. **Consistent aesthetic, varied structure** — all sections share the TRON design language but must not look like clones of each other.
4. **No banned patterns** — side-stripe accents and gradient text are both off. Use corner brackets, background tints, or full-border glows instead.
5. **Evolve within the theme** — the aesthetic is locked, but layout, spacing, and hierarchy can always improve.
